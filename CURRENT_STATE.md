## State-doc maintenance protocol (collapse cadence) — adopted 2026-06-01

**Purpose:** keep this file lean as the arc list grows across nine crops. The "What happened …" entry stack grows ~15–30 lines per session; without collapse it would reach thousands of lines, defeating the file's job (the thing a cold session reads FIRST to orient). The fix is *progressive collapse at checklist-defined boundaries*, not deletion — recent work stays granular, old work flattens to a summary, and nothing load-bearing is ever lost.

**Vocabulary (checklist v1.4 is the source of truth — do NOT conflate these layers):**
- **The arc = Steps 0–11** (the per-crop gold-standard checklist). A single Step can span several sessions (Step 4 currently spans na3b–na3f + the northern_tier reconciliation).
- **Session names (NA/NB/NC/ND)** are this project's chunking convention for a long arc, NOT checklist structure. NA ≈ the scaffolding/authoring stretch; NB ≈ verification grind; etc. The checklist does not define them.

They are NOT stages within a crop arc. *(Lesson logged 2026-06-01: these three layers were conflated in conversation; the checklist settles it — arc = Steps 0–11; A/B/C = migration milestones.)*

**Collapse cadence:**
1. **While a Step is active:** each session appends its full granular "What happened …" entry (detail is wanted at current working depth).

A collapsed summary is then short: a gist + pointers ("findings → ledger; lessons → standing list").
