## 2. ROOT AND CROWN ROT -- THE "NOT COLD" CORRECTION

**The correction is right, it does not overcorrect, and it is written from thyme's own documents.**

Canonical said:

ruled out" and "a documented way" match UMD's modal ("can cause"); winter injury is kept separate,
which matches ISU treating it as a distinct sentence and UMD filing its sentence under winter injury.

> beginner: "Thyme likes dry soil, but a plant left bone dry through a heat wave is the one mites take over. In a long dry spell, give it a single deep soak and then let it dry out again, rather than a splash of water every day."
