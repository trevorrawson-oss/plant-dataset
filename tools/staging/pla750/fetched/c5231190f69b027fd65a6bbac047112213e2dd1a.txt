Err on the side of caution and hold off until later in the month. Brassica and any other transplants will appreciate some shade protection from late afternoon heat and on days with extreme heat.     Keep any seeds planted this month evenly moist to help germination rates.    Continue steady, deep irrigation to summer vegetables. Pick produce regularly to encourage production to the end of the season. Plan when to pull summer vegetables and consider fall legumes and ‘green manure’ plantings to replenish soil nitrogen. Plan crop rotations to discourage common pests within families of vegetables.        YOLO County Vegetables Recommended for August      Plant throughout August     Lettuce – Seed     The following brassicas are best started in shaded seed trays or pots and transplanted out in late September/October.    Broccoli – Seed - shade    Cabbage – Seed - shade     Cauliflower – Seed - shade     Plant in the first three weeks of August     Carrots – Seed – can be very slow to germinate – be patient.
