Rust causes pale green spots on the undersides of leaves.
Pustules containing yellowish spores develop on the upper
leaf surface. Spots may develop on petioles and stems.

Smut attacks all parts of the plants. Elongated or callus-like
pustules develop on the leaf stalks, deforming them. The
pustules break open to discharge spore balls. Remove and
destroy infected plants early in the season.
