Varieties
Conquistador, Florida 683, Summer or Giant Pascal, Utah 52-70

Soil Preferences
Muck soils are ideal, will grow in well drained, fertile, mineral soils (pH 6.0-7.5) with
good water holding capacity.

Optimum Growing Conditions
Celery has exacting climatic requirements, doing best when monthly mean temperature
is 60-70°F. Average high temperature above 75°F in month preceding harvest limits
vegetative growth and quality. Temperatures below 40-55°F induce bolting.
