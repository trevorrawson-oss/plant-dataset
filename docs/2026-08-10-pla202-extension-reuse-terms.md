# PLA-202 annex -- published reuse/copyright terms of the 15 cited extension services

Researched 2026-08-10 (WebSearch/WebFetch, verbatim quotes captured; per-document vs
site-wide locus noted). Context for every entry: **the reuser is a commercial gardening
website**, so noncommercial and education-only grants do NOT cover us.

## The one-line answer

**No institution in the set publishes a grant that covers commercial web
republication.** The kickoff's hope that reuse terms would shrink the problem is
answered: it does not. Benign-class hits stand because they are not protected
expression (facts, quantities, stock phrasing) -- not because reuse is licensed.
Rewrite-class hits cannot be cured by attribution or by pointing at a reuse policy.

Grouping: 6 explicit conditional grants, every one noncommercial/educational-scoped
(UF/IFAS, UC ANR+IPM, WSU, OSU, UGA, MU) - 4 explicit permission-required (USU, UMN,
ISU, CTAHR) - 5 unstated bare-copyright (Clemson, NC State text, Arizona, Arkansas,
TAMU).

Notes that matter for the dataset:

- **Facts and data are not copyrightable** -- rates, dates, thresholds, species lists
  and windows may be stated freely in our own words regardless of any policy above.
  That is copyright law, not a grant from these policies.
- **Linking is universally safe** (UMN and ISU say so explicitly).
- **Images**: NC State Toolbox photos are per-image CC (check each); UC and UF/IFAS
  exclude images from their text licenses. Relevant if artwork sourcing ever considers
  extension photos -- it should not.
- Access caveats recorded by the researcher: extension.iastate.edu and all of
  extension.oregonstate.edu WAF-403 automated fetching (same pattern as our scan's
  blocked documents); ISU's per-document AgDM reprint wording is search-index-only,
  unconfirmed; NC State's university copyright pages are JS-rendered and unreadable to
  the fetcher.
